# After how long is a claim stale?
A machine can stop or lose the network while it holds a claim. Another machine must be able to take the ticket. A short time risks a double run. A long time blocks work.

Options:
1. 10 minutes without a refresh of the stamp. (recommended)
2. 1 hour without a refresh.
3. Never. A person releases the claim by hand.
