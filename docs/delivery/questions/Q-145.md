# What do the roles `lead` and `junior` become?
The new roles have no `lead` (functional lead) and no `junior`. A functional lead owns the backlog and its acceptance. Junior is a level of experience, not a job.

Options:
1. `lead` becomes `po`. `junior` becomes `developer` with `-code.merge` and `-runs.approve`. The Explain action stays for each role. (recommended)
2. Keep `lead` and `junior` as two more roles.
3. Keep `lead` and `junior` as aliases that the reader maps at each load.

## Answer
Option 1: `lead` becomes `po`. `junior` becomes `developer` with `-code.merge` and `-runs.approve`. The Explain action stays for each role. Recommended default, kept: the owner asked to build the rest with sensible defaults (2026-11-06). The owner can change it.
