# Do rules 3.1 and 3.2 of the constitution change?
Rule 3.1 lists PM, architect, developer, QA, BA, functional lead and junior. Rule 3.2 says that roles change the order and the home view only. Rights per role break rule 3.2. A change of the constitution needs the owner (ADR 0019).

Options:
1. 3.1 lists the nine roles of `team-rights`. 3.2 says: no role loses access to a view; roles change the order, the home view, the Today panels and the rights; a right turns a control off, never a view. (recommended)
2. Keep the constitution and drop the rights per role.

## Answer
Option 1. The owner's goal (2026-11-06) asks for access and permissions per profile for the whole project team. The constitution changes rules 3.1 and 3.2 in this change.
