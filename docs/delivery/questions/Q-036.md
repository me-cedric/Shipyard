# May a person archive a change with open work items?
Archive moves a change out of the active list. The plan says to archive when the work items are done. A change sometimes ships with a work item dropped.

Options:
1. Archive needs all work items done or closed. The person confirms a list of the open ones to override. (recommended)
2. Archive needs all work items done. No override.
3. Archive has no check.
