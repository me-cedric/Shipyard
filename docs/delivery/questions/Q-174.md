# What does Go on do when a budget is reached?
The design has a row Then with Stop and ask and Go on. A spend control must not go away.

Options:
1. Go on makes the budget a warning only. Pause and Ask me stay.
2. Go on stops at a hard cap.
3. Remove Go on.

## Answer
Option 1: no hard cap. Go on makes the budget a warning only, and the agent can spend without limit. The choices Pause and Ask me stay. Stop all agents, the stall timeout and the max turns stay. Owner choice, 2026-11-20. (2026-11-20)
