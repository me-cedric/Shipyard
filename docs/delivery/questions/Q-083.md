# Where does the Health fix for child projects run?
REQ-018 runs a Health fix on the children that the person selects. Settings / Extensions deploys to the selected children with `child_deploy`. Settings / Health belongs to `add-projects` and shows no child selection yet.

Options:
1. Keep the deploy in Settings / Extensions. Health links to it in a later stage. (recommended)
2. Add a child selection to the Health fix in Settings / Health.
