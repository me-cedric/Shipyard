# Where do the Legacy ticket links, the budget and the delivery notes come from?
The links come from docs/delivery/tickets.json (read only). The budget bar uses the estimate and the time spent that the tracker gives. The notes are the files of docs/delivery/impacts (front matter title, date, base, audience). Jira create uses the issue types of tickets.config.json.

1. Read the Legacy files in place and write none of them. (chosen)
2. Convert them into Shipyard files.
