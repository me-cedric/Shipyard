# Where do the quality and impact reports live in the repository?
REQ-028 and REQ-033 keep the reports as repository text files. The code writes `.shipyard/quality/<kind>-<name>.yaml` and `.shipyard/impacts/<date>-<slug>.plan.md` with `.digest.md`. A report makes the working copy dirty until a person commits it. The scanner skips the quality folder.

Options:
1. Keep these folders and let the person commit them. (recommended)
2. Write the reports to `docs/quality/` and `docs/impacts/`, next to the other documents.
3. Add the folders to `.gitignore` and keep the reports on this machine only (a teammate sees no result after a clone).

## Answer
Keep these folders and let the person commit them.
