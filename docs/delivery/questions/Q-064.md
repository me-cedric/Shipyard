# May the estimate annex show a day rate when the sheet is not sealed?
REQ-014 forbids the day rate of a sealed sheet. A sheet holds no day rate: the rates are in the team file. The export lists the task, the role and the days, and it never reads the rates. An unsealed team could show a rate in the annex.

Options:
1. The annex never holds a day rate, sealed or not. (recommended)
2. The annex holds a day rate for each role when the team file is not sealed.
3. The annex holds a day rate only after a click of the person for each export.
