# Does the tour replace the manual?
The owner asked to replace the guidebook if the tour is very good. The manual (Knowledge / Manual, `docs/guide/`) has nine pages of reference text: the shortcuts, the words table, each tab of each space, the safety rules. A tour of six short steps cannot hold that text, and the palette action Open the manual for this page and F1 depend on it.

Options:
1. The tour replaces the manual.
2. The tour stays short and its last step opens the manual.

## Answer

Option 2 (claude, 2026-11-05, from the evidence above): the tour is the first minute, the manual is the reference. No ADR, because nothing is replaced. The owner can reopen the question.
