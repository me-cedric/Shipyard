# Seven spaces replace forty routes
## Context and problem statement
Legacy has 40 routes, 12 sidebar groups and 4 filters that hide pages.
## Decision drivers
- One way to do each thing.
- Every role sees every view.
## Considered options
- Keep pages, hide by role (Legacy).
- Seven spaces with tabs: Home, Design, Specs, Work, Factory, Code, Knowledge, plus Settings.
## Decision outcome
Chosen option: "Seven spaces", because tabs inside a space replace overlapping pages, and roles change only the order and the home view.
### Consequences
- The parity table in the master plan is the acceptance list for W9.
