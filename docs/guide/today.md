# Today

Today tells you what to do now. The switch at the top has three views: Needs you, News and Activity. The page opens on Needs you. Today is the only place that shows a count in the rail.

## Needs you

The title gives the count of the things that need you. One card shows the first thing: who asks, the feature, one large line that says what to do, and the answer.

- Answer a question: press a digit, then Enter. A question that takes a free answer has the field Another answer: type your own answer there.
- Approve or reject a change. Read a plan, then approve it or reject it with a note.
- Open the pull request of a review. An agent review draft that waits has a link to its pull request.
- Mark a message as read.
- A change that the automation switches do not allow waits here for your click. The writes of one run are one question that names each effect, with Each step, Not yet and Do not ask again for this recipe. See the page Flow, Approvals.
- A stopped agent shows one main action: Reply, Unblock, Add budget, Try again, Resume or Close as done.

Later moves the card to the end. A short queue under the card lists the next three things, and "N more" shows the rest in place. Click one to answer it first.

The card shows what you can act on: the approvals that your rights allow, the questions of your tickets or of your role, and the reviews. Everyone shows the things of the whole team, with the controls that you cannot use turned off. With Everyone on, the count of the title can differ from the count of the menu.

With nothing at all to show, one action opens Flow / List.

## The rest of the page

- Working now lists the runs that work on your tickets or wait for you, with Stop, and the spend of today.
- While a setup step is open, one small setup card shows the next step and how many steps are done. Continue opens Setup / Start (see Getting started, First launch).
- On a work day with no time logged, one slim card reminds you to log your time, with Log. It is not in the count.
- Panels for my role opens the panels of your role as small cards, each with Open. A panel of a place that you cannot see stays hidden.
- On the page of the panels, one chip for each role shows the panels of that role, and the panels of the other roles are listed under them. The viewer chip says "I only look". All role views opens Setup / Role views.
- The same page holds My work, the tickets that the issue tracker gives to you, grouped as In progress, To do and Done in 7 days, each with its status and Start run, and Since you were away, what happened on your tickets and runs since the last time you opened Today, newest first.
- A product owner sees the cards of the plan on Today itself, above Working now.

The menu bar panel shows the same things that need you.

## News

News holds the notifications. The title gives their count. Each row shows who, the title, the feature and one action for its kind: Review, Budget, Sync again or Open. A control removes the row. Older rows sit behind Older news. Mark all as read and Clear act on the list. No other page holds a list of notifications.

Tell me when has five switches: An agent needs me, An agent finishes, A person asks for a review, A budget is near its limit and The sync has a problem. More in Setup opens Setup / Me, which has one switch for each source of notifications.

## Activity

Activity lists the live runs and the open sessions, each with Stop.
