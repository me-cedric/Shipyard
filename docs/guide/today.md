# Today

Today tells you what to do now. The switch at the top has three sections: Needs you, News and Activity. A dot on Today in the menu marks unread news, and a live mark shows while a run or a session works.

## Needs you

The title gives the count of the things that need you. One card shows the first thing: who asks, for which feature, and the answer.

- Answer a question: press a digit, then Enter, or type your own answer.
- Approve or reject a change. Read a plan, then approve it or reject it with a note.
- Open the pull request of a review. An agent review draft that waits has a link to its pull request.
- Mark a message as read.
- A change that the automation switches do not allow waits here for your click. The writes of one run are one question that names each effect, with Each step, Not yet and Do not ask again for this recipe. See the page Flow, Approvals.
- A stopped agent shows one main action: Reply, Unblock, Add budget, Try again, Resume or Close as done.

Later moves the card to the end. The list under the card holds the next things: click one to answer it first.

The card shows what you can act on: the approvals that your rights allow, the questions of your tickets or of your role, and the reviews. Everyone shows the things of the whole team, with the controls that you cannot use turned off. With Everyone on, the count of the title can differ from the count of the menu.

With nothing at all to show, one action opens Flow / List.

## The rest of the page

- Working now lists the runs that work on your tickets or wait for you, with Stop, and the spend of today.
- While an item is to do, one Get started checklist shows (see Getting started, First launch).
- On a work day with no time logged, a card reminds you to log your time. It is not in the count.
- The panels of your role show what your role watches, each with Open. Pick a role to see the panels of another role. A panel of a place that you cannot see stays hidden.
- My items holds the tickets that the issue tracker gives to you, grouped as In progress, To do and Done in the last 7 days. Each row has its status and Start run.
- Since you were away lists what happened on your tickets and runs since the last time you opened Today, newest first.

The menu bar panel shows the same things that need you.

## News

News holds the notifications. The title gives their count. Each row has its action and a control to remove it. Mark all as read and Clear act on the list. No other page holds a list of notifications. Click an entry to open its run, pull request, Health page or draft.

Setup / Me has one switch for each source of notifications.

## Activity

Activity lists the live runs and the open sessions, each with Stop.
