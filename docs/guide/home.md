# Home

Home shows what to do now and your projects.

## Today

Today tells you what to do now. The switch at the top has three views: Today, News and Activity. With nothing at all to show, one action opens the backlog.

- The title gives the count of the things that need you. One card shows the first thing: who asks, for which feature, and the answer. Answer a question: press a digit, then Enter, or type your own answer. Approve or reject a change. Read a plan, then approve it or reject it with a note. Open the pull request of a review. Mark a message as read. A change that the automation switches do not allow waits here for your click. The writes of one run are one question that names each effect, with Each step, Not yet and Do not ask again for this recipe. A stopped agent shows one main action: Reply, Unblock, Add budget, Try again, Resume or Close as done.
- Later moves the card to the end. The list under the card holds the next things: click one to answer it first. Everyone shows the things of the whole team.
- Working now lists the runs that work on your tickets or wait for you, with Stop, and the spend of today.
- While an item is to do, one Get started checklist shows (see Getting started, First launch).
- On a work day with no time logged, a card reminds you to log your time. It is not in the count.
- The panels of your role show what your role watches, each with Open. Pick a role to see the panels of another role. A panel of a place that you cannot see stays hidden.
- My work holds the tickets that the issue tracker gives to you, grouped as In progress, To do and Done in the last 7 days. Each row has its status and Start run.
- Since you were away lists what happened on your tickets and runs since the last time you opened Today, newest first.

The old addresses of Inbox and My work open Today. The menu bar panel shows the same things that need you. The tiles that you pin in Knowledge show at the top of Knowledge, then Dashboards.

## News

News holds the notifications: the title gives their count, each row has its action and a control to remove it, Mark all as read and Clear act on the list. No other page holds a list of notifications. A dot on Today in the menu marks unread news.

## Activity

Activity lists the live runs and the open sessions, each with Stop. The old address of Activity opens it.

## Projects

Projects lists the projects on this computer. Open it from the project name in the top bar, then Projects. It shows the open project, the projects opened before, and the ways to start one. The old address of Home / Projects opens it.

1. Click a project to open it.
2. Click Open a folder to add a project that is on your disk.
3. Click Create to start a new project. The app proposes a folder and a key.

With no project open, every tab of the app shows the same page: Open a folder, Clone a repository and New project.

In Settings, then Project, you can rename the project and give it a logo. The logo shows in the top bar. The same page restores saved changes (stashes), shows what they hold, and brings a branch that lags behind the server up to date. In Settings, then Health, Inspect shows the branch and the changes of a linked project, and Open in mail opens your mail app with your feedback.
