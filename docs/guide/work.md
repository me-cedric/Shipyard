# Work

Work shows the tickets of the issue tracker and the plan of the team. The app keeps a copy of the tickets, so Work also opens without a network.

## Backlog

The old address of Backlog opens Flow, in the List view, which holds the same tickets and filters.

Backlog lists the tickets by state. Filter by state, person, label and run.

- Import: the app shows the count first. It writes nothing before you confirm.
- Assign: pick a person. The app proposes a person by skills and free time. The choice is yours.
- Mark for the agent: one click gives the ticket to the agents.

## Board

The old address of Board opens Flow, which shows each feature once in five stages (Idea, Spec, Build, Review, Ready). A card shows the title, who works on it and its state.

Board shows one column for each state. Move a card with the mouse or the keyboard. When the issue tracker refuses the move, the card goes back and shows the reason.

## Plan

Plan is a place of its own, with the sections Timeline, People, Estimates and Time. The old address of Work / Plan opens Plan / Timeline.

Timeline draws the schedule of a lot as a Gantt chart, from an estimate sheet, the team and the calendar. The title gives the day that the release ends. Dates and days off sets the working days and the public holidays. Export the chart as a picture.

Send to the issue tracker shows a preview first: New, Changed and Stays the same, each with its count. Nothing goes to the issue tracker before you click Apply.

When a person has too much work, one card names the problem and lists proposals: a task goes to a person with room, or it starts later. Apply does one proposal. Rebalance does each of them.

People shows each person with the load against the capacity of each week, the tasks and their dates. A person with too much work has the proposals there too.

## Estimates

Estimates is in Plan. It holds one sheet for each lot. Each row has a low, a likely and a high estimate. The app computes the price, the cost and the margin from the day rates.

- Duplicate, rename, freeze or delete a sheet from its menu.
- Seal the project with a passphrase to hide the day rates in the files.
- Ask an agent to estimate: select tasks, then click it. The agent proposes a Best, a Likely and a Worst value with its assumptions. A value enters the sheet only when you click Accept. Discard drops the proposal. The agent shows in Crew while it works. Stop ends it, and the sheet keeps its values. The action is off on a sealed sheet and on a frozen sheet.

## Team

Team is now Crew. Its old address opens Crew. Crew shows each person as a card: the current work, the role, the days a week, the skills and the day rate. Click a card to change the person. Crew / Rights sets the rights. See the page Team and rights.

## Time

Time is in Plan. A week grid shows a row for each ticket and a column for each day. Type the hours in a cell to add or change an entry. Empty the cell to delete the entry, after you confirm. A cell with two entries or more shows the sum: change them in the list of the month.

Time lists the time that each person spent, by month. The app sends a time entry to the issue tracker only after your click.

Click Read the month to read the worklogs of the month from the issue tracker, beside your entries. This changes nothing.

A role with fewer than three members with a day rate shows no cost. With one or two rates, a cost would show the rate of a person.
